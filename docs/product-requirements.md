# Inventory Risk Assistant

## Overview

Inventory Risk Assistant is an AI-powered inventory planning platform designed to help inventory analysts identify stockout risks, prioritize replenishment decisions, and understand inventory health through data-driven insights.

The application analyzes SKU-level inventory data, highlights products requiring immediate attention, and provides an AI assistant capable of answering inventory-related questions using business context.

---

## Problem Statement

Inventory analysts often work with large spreadsheets and ERP reports containing thousands of SKUs.

Identifying which products require replenishment is time-consuming and prone to human error.

The goal of this product is to reduce decision-making time and improve inventory prioritization by automatically identifying risk patterns and surfacing actionable insights.

---

## Target User

Inventory Planner

Responsibilities:

* Monitor inventory health
* Prevent stockouts
* Optimize replenishment decisions
* Reduce excess inventory
* Identify supplier-related risks
